Flux Ensemble

1 Jan 2022

Up to four sites. Sections are variable families, so one site fills every desk and several sites share them, west to east.

Seen from above. Left and right here is left and right in the sound. Colour is the site.

One note per day. L0 is the data used as a waveform, L1 a tone gliding with it, L2 notes on the harmony. No wavelets here: this page plays daily values directly. Download MIDI writes the whole year at this tempo for a DAW or MuseScore: the notes of every seat you can hear, on the nearest equal-tempered key. L0 and L1 have no notes, so those seats are left out. Daily data: ICOS RI et al., 2026, Ecosystem final quality (L2) product in ETC-Archive and associated formats, INTERIM release 2026-2, doi.org/10.18160/JX1S-VXZQ, CC BY 4.0. Land outline: Natural Earth. Only sites with at least 95% of every variable that year are offered, and a day with no data is silent. Instruments are synthesised sketches, not recordings.
