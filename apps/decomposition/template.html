Half an hour of 20 Hz data from the FR-Gri tower, split into wavelet scales with FluxScale. Turn scales on and off: the picture, the chart and the sound follow. L0 is the waveform itself, L1 the same scales moved into hearing range, L2 the music. Heard frequency shows where each level puts the sound; Period shows the scales themselves. Download MIDI takes the L2 music of the chosen signal to a DAW or MuseScore, with a track per scale and per melody line.

A whole day of w × CO₂ at L2

Local correlation between wind and CO₂ over 30-minute windows. Inside the band it is too weak to pull the instruments together. From mid-morning they lock into consonant, opposite motion over a minor bass.
